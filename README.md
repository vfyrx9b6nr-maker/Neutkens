This repository contains the source code for **https://woutneutkens.nl** – an essay on constructing Heaven from reliable rules, rejecting completed infinities, and uniting Wolfram Physics, Integrated Information Theory (IIT), and an ethics grounded in happiness and suffering.

## 🧭 Contents

- `index.html` – the full essay, single page, minimal design  
- `styles.css` – custom styling (clean, readable, focused on text)  
- No external frameworks, no trackers, no JavaScript clutter  

## 📖 Abstract (from the essay)

> *"By rejecting completed infinities, we can construct a more reliable foundation not only for physics and consciousness, but for ethics – and ultimately, for something we may dare to call Heaven."*

The essay builds three pillars:

1. **Physics** – A single computational rule, à la Stephen Wolfram, evolving a hypergraph without a completed infinite Ruliad.  
2. **Consciousness** – Tononi's IIT (Φ as integrated information), bridging causal structure to experience.  
3. **Ethics** – Valence conditioned by evolution, but open to re‑conditioning toward inexhaustible happiness.  

The conclusion: Heaven is not a destination but a direction – an open‑ended accumulation of harmonious complexity, made possible by safety, science, and cooperation.
